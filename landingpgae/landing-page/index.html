Launch Career Paths Through Trusted Early Experience

Handshake connects students, employers, and administrators in one verified platform for internships, entry-level jobs, applications, messaging, reviews, and moderation.

Problem

Students and employers need a safer starting point

Early-career hiring often feels confusing, untrusted, and full of incomplete information.

Fake or unclear listings

Students need a way to avoid suspicious postings and roles that are not truly beginner-friendly.

Unverified accounts

Employers and students both need trust signals before they invest time applying or hiring.

Fragmented workflows

Applications, messages, reviews, and moderation often live in separate tools with little visibility.

Solution

A verified platform for internships, entry-level jobs, and moderation

Handshake brings the entire early-career hiring flow into one polished, role-based experience.

Verified Student

Students can browse and apply with confidence

Students can save jobs, manage applications, view employer profiles, message companies, and build trust through verification badges and profile details.

Verified Company

Employers can post and manage roles responsibly

Verified employers can publish internships and entry-level jobs, review applicants, manage conversations, and maintain a stronger presence.

Key Features

Built from features already in the prototype

The current app already supports browsing, applying, messaging, saving, verification, reviews, reports, and dashboards.

Job browsing and filtering

Search openings by title, skill, and category with a clean split-view browsing experience.

Save jobs for later

Students can bookmark roles and return to them through their saved jobs area.

Application tracking

Applicants can follow statuses such as Applied, Under Review, Interview, Accepted, Rejected, and Closed.

Messaging

Students and employers can continue conversations in a built-in messages area.

Verification flow

Pending users can upload documents and admins can approve, reject, or request more information.

Reviews and reputation

Students and employers can rate each other after working together to strengthen trust.

Target Users

Designed for three clear roles

The interface is role-based so each user sees tools that match their responsibilities.

Students

Students browse internships and entry-level jobs, upload verification documents, manage their profile, and apply once verified.

Employers

Employers submit business verification documents, post jobs, review applicants, and manage their company profile.

Admins

Admins review verification requests, inspect user details, manage reports, and monitor platform activity from one dashboard.
