# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title
Student Internship & Entry-Level Job Platform

## 2. Target User / Customer
- Primary user: University student or fresh graduate searching for internships or entry-level jobs (Persona: Ploy)
- Secondary user: Employer, startup, or SME recruiting early-career talent (Persona: Niran)
- Decision-maker or admin role, if any: Platform Admin, who reviews student/company verification requests and handles reported job posts (Admin Verification Page)

> Assumption: This scenario covers both personas, since the platform is a two-sided marketplace where value depends on both roles participating. If a single-persona version is needed for a specific demo moment, this can be split later.

## 3. Situation / Context
Ploy is in her final year at university and has started looking for an internship before graduation. She has been relying on scattered university Facebook groups and LINE chats, where postings are inconsistent, often missing eligibility details, and hard to verify as genuine.

At the same time, Niran runs recruitment for a small startup that wants to bring on an intern. He does not have the budget for a premium job board and has previously received applications from students who did not meet the basic eligibility criteria, wasting time on unqualified applicants.

Both discover the Student Internship & Entry-Level Job Platform: Ploy through a university career-office referral, and Niran through a startup community recommendation.

## 4. Pain Point
- Student pain point: Difficulty finding trustworthy, beginner-friendly postings; no clear way to confirm a company is legitimate before applying; no centralized way to track application status across multiple job posts.
- Employer pain point: Difficulty reaching genuine early-career candidates efficiently; risk of unverified or unqualified applicants; no simple system to manage and update applicant status.

## 5. Product Introduction
Ploy is introduced to the platform through her university's career services page, which links to the platform's login screen. After a simulated login and role selection, she is guided to complete her student profile and verification.

Niran is introduced to the platform through a partner startup accelerator's recommended tools list. After logging in as an Employer, he is guided to set up a company profile and submit verification before he is permitted to post jobs.

## 6. Value Message
For university students and fresh graduates who experience difficulty finding trustworthy, beginner-friendly internship and entry-level opportunities, our Student Internship & Entry-Level Job Platform helps them discover, apply to, and track verified job opportunities with confidence by providing verified company profiles, beginner-friendly filters, resume-based applications, and a real-time application tracker.

For SMEs and startups who experience difficulty reaching genuine early-career talent, the same platform helps them recruit verified, beginner-friendly candidates efficiently by requiring company verification before posting and providing tools to review, filter, and manage applicants in one place.

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment
**To Ploy (student):**
"Instead of scrolling through group chats hoping a posting is real, you log in, verify with your university email, and browse jobs that are already tagged beginner-friendly. Every company you see has a verified badge, so you know it's legitimate before you apply. Once you apply, you can track your status right on your dashboard instead of guessing."

**To Niran (employer):**
"You register your company and go through a quick verification step — once that's approved, you can post your internship listing. Every applicant who reaches you has already gone through student verification, so you're not sorting through spam. You can filter applicants, review resumes, and update their status, all from one dashboard."

## 8. Expected User Action
- Student: Complete verification, upload resume, browse and apply to a beginner-friendly job, track application status
- Employer: Complete company verification, post a job listing, review and manage applicants
