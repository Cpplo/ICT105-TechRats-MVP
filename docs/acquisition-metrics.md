# Acquisition Metrics

## Purpose
Define how the group will measure early user interest and landing page performance for the Student Internship & Entry-Level Job Platform.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Landing page views | Number of people who opened the landing page (awareness) | Count page views using form logs, script counter, or manual tracking | Landing page / spreadsheet | At least 20 views across all channels |
| M-02 | CTA clicks ("Try Demo") | Number of users who clicked the main CTA button (action) | CTA clicks / page views | Landing page script / manual count | At least 25% click rate |
| M-03 | Demo attempts | Number of users who opened or tested the prototype after clicking CTA (action) | Count demo/prototype visits | Prototype link / form | At least 10 attempts |
| M-04 | Feedback responses | Number of users who submitted feedback after trying the demo (feedback) | Count submitted feedback forms | Google Form / spreadsheet | At least 5 responses |
| M-05 | Interest conversion rate | Percentage of visitors who completed the target action, e.g. tried the demo (conversion) | (Demo attempts / page views) × 100 | Spreadsheet | At least 15% conversion |

## Interpretation
If the metrics are weak, the group will:
- Rework the headline or subheadline if landing page views are high but CTA clicks are low (M-02 weak) — the message may not be clear enough.
- Simplify the demo flow or fix broken links if CTA clicks are high but demo attempts are low (M-03 weak) — users are dropping off before trying the prototype.
- Shorten or simplify the feedback form if demo attempts are high but feedback responses are low (M-04 weak).
- Test a different channel or message angle (from `go-to-market-plan.md`) if landing page views are low overall (M-01 weak) — the current channels may not be reaching the right audience.
- Revisit the value proposition and Key Features section in `landing-page-content.md` if the overall conversion rate (M-05) stays below target after other fixes.
