# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name
Student Internship & Entry-Level Job Platform

## Target Early Users
The first users are university students and fresh graduates actively looking for internships or entry-level roles, particularly those who have felt unsure whether a listing or company is legitimate. A secondary early-user group is SMEs and startups that want to recruit beginner talent but lack an easy way to reach verified student candidates.

## Main Value Proposition
Students find verified, beginner-friendly internships and entry-level jobs, while employers recruit verified early-career talent through a secure, transparent platform.

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Class/course chat group (e.g. Telegram/WhatsApp) | Students | Stop guessing which internship listings are real | Click demo link | Clicks/demo views | Students already check these groups daily for opportunities |
| Campus poster with QR code | Students on campus | Apply to verified internships in one place | Scan QR to visit landing page | QR scans/landing page visits | Reaches students physically near career services or notice boards |
| University career center / student club email or Instagram | Students, career-focused clubs | Verified companies, no more guessing | Visit landing page, follow for updates | Link clicks/story clicks | Career centers and clubs already have student trust and reach |
| LinkedIn post/outreach | Employers (SMEs, startups) | Recruit verified beginner talent, faster | Click demo/signup link | Clicks/demo requests | LinkedIn is where SMEs already look for hiring tools and talent |

## Launch Sequence
1. Prepare landing page and demo link, and confirm the CTA button works end-to-end.
2. Share the landing page link in the class chat group and post the campus QR poster.
3. Send the landing page to the university career center/student club and publish the LinkedIn post for employer-side reach.
4. Track responses (clicks, QR scans, demo visits) using a spreadsheet or Google Form log.
5. Collect informal feedback from early visitors (students and any employer contacts).
6. Revise the landing page message, CTA, or a specific prototype feature based on what users struggled with or asked about.

## Risk and Mitigation
- **Risk:** Students may think the platform is a real, fully live service and expect real job placements. **Mitigation:** Clearly label the landing page and demo as a prototype/pilot, and avoid promising outcomes the prototype can't demonstrate.
- **Risk:** Low response from the employer side, since SMEs are harder to reach than students. **Mitigation:** Focus outreach effort primarily on student channels first, treating employer/LinkedIn outreach as a secondary, lower-volume channel.
- **Risk:** QR code or demo link fails during in-person promotion (e.g. poster). **Mitigation:** Test the link and QR code before printing/posting, and keep a backup shortened URL visible on the poster.
