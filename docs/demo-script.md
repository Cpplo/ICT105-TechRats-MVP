# Demo Script

## Demo Objective
Demonstrate how the Student Internship & Entry-Level Job Platform helps university students discover, apply to, and track verified internship and entry-level opportunities, while enabling employers to recruit verified beginner-friendly talent — showing the full pathway from landing page to application outcome for both user roles.

## Demo Flow
| Step | Screen / Feature | What Presenter Says | Requirement ID | Expected User Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Homepage | Introduce the problem: students struggle to find trustworthy beginner-friendly jobs, and employers struggle to reach verified early-career talent. Introduce both target users. | FR-01 | Sets context for why the platform exists |
| 2 | Login / Role Selection | Show simulated login and role selection into Student or Employer dashboard. | FR-01, FR-02 | Users enter the platform quickly without a real account barrier |
| 3 | Student Profile & Verification | Create/edit student profile, submit university email for verification, upload resume and supporting documents. | FR-03, FR-05, FR-06 | Student establishes a trustworthy, verifiable profile |
| 4 | Job Listing Page | Browse and search jobs, apply beginner-friendly and eligibility filters. | FR-11, FR-13 | Student quickly narrows down to suitable, beginner-friendly roles |
| 5 | Job Detail Page | Show job description, company profile, verified badge, eligibility. | FR-14, FR-15, FR-29 | Student confirms legitimacy of employer before applying |
| 6 | Job Application Submission | Apply using resume and selected supporting documents; show confirmation message. | FR-16, FR-24 | Student completes application with confidence |
| 7 | Application Tracker | Show application status (Applied, Under Review, Interview, etc.) on Student Dashboard. | FR-17, FR-12 | Student can follow progress without needing to ask the employer directly |
| 8 | Messaging | Student sends a message to the employer about the role. | FR-21 | Student can clarify details before/after applying |
| 9 | Employer Registration & Verification | Switch to Employer role: create company profile, submit verification info, show Pending status. | FR-07, FR-08 | Employer establishes a trustworthy, verified company presence |
| 10 | Job Posting (Blocked Until Verified) | Attempt to post a job before verification is approved, showing the block; then post after verification. | FR-10, FR-30 | Demonstrates platform trust/safety rule for students |
