# Landing Page Content

## Project Title
Student Internship & Entry-Level Job Platform

## Target User
University students and fresh graduates searching for trustworthy internships or entry-level jobs, and SMEs/startups looking to recruit verified beginner talent.

## Headline
Find Verified Internships. Recruit Verified Talent.

## Subheadline
A platform where students discover beginner-friendly internship and entry-level opportunities from verified companies, and employers recruit early-career talent through a secure, transparent hiring process.

## Problem Statement
Students and fresh graduates often struggle to tell which internship and entry-level listings are legitimate and beginner-friendly, making it hard to apply with confidence. At the same time, employers — especially SMEs and startups — lack a simple way to reach and verify early-career candidates. This platform addresses that gap by verifying both students and companies before they can post, apply, or connect.

*(Note: this section is based on the validated problem statement from the MVP Decision Rule, not direct Lab 03 respondent quotes. Share the Lab 03 evidence file to strengthen this with specific findings.)*

## Solution Description
The platform gives students a verified profile with resume/portfolio upload, filterable job search, job detail viewing, application submission, and an application tracker. Employers get a verified company profile, job posting (unlocked only after verification), an applicant management view, and messaging. Both roles are supported by dashboards summarizing status, activity, and next actions.

## Benefits
- **For students:** Confidence that listed jobs and companies are verified, faster filtering to beginner-friendly roles, and clear visibility into application progress instead of silence after applying.
- **For employers:** Access to a pool of verified student candidates, a simple applicant review workflow, and credibility from displaying a verified company badge.

## Responsible Data Message
This platform collects only the information needed to verify identity and process applications — such as name, university/company details, resume, and contact information. Uploaded documents (resumes, portfolios, verification files) are treated as protected data and are not shared beyond what's needed for the application or verification process. No unnecessary personal or sensitive data is requested.
