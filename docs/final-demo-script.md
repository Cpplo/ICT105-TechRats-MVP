# Final Demo Script
Recommended duration: 5-7 minutes

## 1. Opening
Hello everyone. Our project is the **Student Internship & Entry-Level Job Platform**.

The problem we are solving is that university students and fresh graduates often struggle to find trustworthy internships and entry-level jobs, while employers struggle to find and manage beginner-level candidates efficiently. Existing platforms are often too broad, too experienced-focused, or lack trust signals.

Our target users are:
- University students
- Fresh graduates
- SMEs and startups
- Employers
- Admin users for verification and report handling

Our value proposition is simple:
- Students get verified, beginner-friendly opportunities
- Employers get a trust-based hiring workflow
- Admins help maintain platform safety through verification and moderation

---

## 2. User Scenario
Imagine a final-year student who wants an internship but is not sure which jobs are truly beginner-friendly. They need to check if a company is verified, see the job details clearly, and apply with confidence.

At the same time, an employer wants to post an internship, review applicants, and update application status without dealing with unqualified or suspicious applicants.

Our prototype supports both sides through a clean, role-based workflow.

---

## 3. Prototype Walkthrough

| Step | Screen/Feature | What to Demonstrate | Requirement ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Homepage | Show the project title, target users, problem statement, and main call-to-action | FR-01 |
| 2 | Input form | Show simulated login or submit a profile / verification request / job application form depending on role | FR-03 |
| 3 | Record list | Show stored or simulated records such as jobs, applications, or applicants | FR-05 |
| 4 | Search/filter | Search or filter jobs by beginner-friendly status, skills, category, or eligibility | FR-06 |
| 5 | Detail view | Open a job detail or company profile page and show full information | FR-07 |
| 6 | Admin/status | Show verification review, report handling, or application status updates | FR-08, FR-09 |
| 7 | Dashboard/metrics | Show summary cards, counts, or landing page acquisition metrics | FR-11 |

### Suggested demo flow talk track
- Start at the homepage and briefly explain what the platform does.
- Move to login or the relevant form and show the role-based workflow.
- Open the record list or job list to show the stored sample data.
- Use search or filters to find beginner-friendly roles.
- Open a detail page to show the full opportunity and verification badge.
- Show admin or status handling to explain trust and moderation.
- End with a dashboard or metric view to show platform summary and tracking.

---

## 4. Evidence and Validation
We tested the prototype with representative users in student, employer, and admin-style tasks.

What users tested:
- Browsing jobs
- Viewing job details
- Checking verification badges
- Applying for jobs
- Saving jobs
- Tracking application status
- Reviewing applicants
- Updating application status
- Reviewing verification requests and reports

Evidence collected showed that:
- Students understood the browsing and job detail flow
- Beginner-friendly labels helped users identify suitable roles
- Company verification badges increased trust
- Application tracking was useful
- Employers found applicant review and status updates helpful
- Admin verification and report handling need clearer labels and visibility

---

## 5. Closing
In conclusion, our final prototype demonstrates a trust-based platform for internships and entry-level jobs that helps students find suitable opportunities and helps employers recruit beginner talent more efficiently.
